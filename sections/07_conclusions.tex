\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
We have proposed a fully specified, valve-free FSI benchmark for an idealised
left ventricle that adds blood flow to the cardiac elastodynamics benchmark of
\citet{Arostica2025}, as two separate problems --- passive filling and active
contraction --- each with a solid-only counterpart already solved by nine
codes. \RES{Main quantitative findings: effect of the fluid relative to the
solid-only solutions; required resolution; observed orders; recommended
stabilisation and coupling settings; converged QoIs with uncertainty.} The
geometry, meshes, case files and reference data are openly available, and we
invite other groups to submit solutions \TODO{(community comparison, as in AR25)}.
