\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
We have defined two valve-free FSI problems in an idealised left ventricle
--- pressure-driven passive deformation and activation-driven contraction
--- that extend the cardiac elastodynamics benchmark of
\citet{Arostica2025}, each with a solid-only counterpart already solved by
nine codes, and an executable reference configuration whose coupling
sequence, residual definitions, tolerances and stabilisation are stated
explicitly. \RES{Main quantitative findings: coupled vs.\ solid-only
responses; required resolution; observed spatial and temporal orders;
recommended settings; QoIs with estimated uncertainty.} The cases,
forcing tables and data are to be released at a fixed commit with an
archival DOI, and other groups are invited to submit solutions.
