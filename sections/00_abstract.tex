\begin{abstract}
Fluid--structure interaction (FSI) models of the left ventricle (LV) are
increasingly used to study intraventricular haemodynamics, yet published
studies rarely report the information needed to reproduce them: ventricular
dimensions, constitutive parameters, the spatial and temporal schemes, the
coupling algorithm and its tolerances, and evidence of mesh and time-step
convergence. Unlike structural cardiac mechanics, for which community
benchmarks now exist \citep{Land2015,Arostica2025}, there is no fully specified
benchmark for ventricular FSI.
We propose one. The problem extends the monoventricular elastodynamics
benchmark of \citet{Arostica2025} --- an idealised truncated-ellipsoid LV with
an analytical fibre field, a visco-active Holzapfel--Ogden myocardium and
pericardial Robin boundary conditions --- by filling its cavity with an
incompressible Newtonian fluid. The cardiac cycle is driven without valves:
a passive filling phase through an inflow port is followed by an active
contraction phase that ejects through an outflow port, both with analytically
prescribed flow-rate waveforms. The benchmark is organised hierarchically:
(i) solid-only verification against the multi-code reference solutions of
\citet{Arostica2025}, (ii) moving-domain CFD with prescribed wall motion,
(iii) coupled passive filling and (iv) the full filling--contraction cycle.
For each step we define quantities of interest --- cavity volume and pressure,
pressure--volume loop, material-point displacements, intraventricular
pressure difference, kinetic energy and vortex metrics --- and report
systematic mesh-, time-step- and scheme-sensitivity studies with
grid-convergence indices.
Reference solutions are computed with the finite-volume toolbox solids4foam
\RES{key numbers: e.g.\ converged ejection fraction, peak pressure, GCI}.
All geometry, meshes, case files and reference data are released openly, and a
minimum reporting checklist for ventricular FSI studies is proposed.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item A fully specified, valve-free FSI benchmark of an idealised left ventricle.
\item Extends the Ar\'ostica et al.\ (2025) cardiac elastodynamics benchmark to FSI.
\item Hierarchical steps: solid-only, prescribed-motion CFD, filling, full cycle.
\item Mesh, time-step and scheme sensitivity with grid-convergence indices.
\item Open meshes, case files, reference data and a reporting checklist.
\end{highlights}
