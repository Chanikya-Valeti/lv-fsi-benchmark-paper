\begin{abstract}
Fluid--structure interaction (FSI) models of the left ventricle (LV) are
increasingly used to study intraventricular haemodynamics, yet published
studies rarely report the information needed to reproduce them: ventricular
dimensions, constitutive parameters, spatial and temporal schemes,
stabilisation, the coupling algorithm and its tolerances, and evidence of mesh
and time-step convergence. Community benchmarks now exist for cardiac solid
mechanics \citep{Land2015,Arostica2025} but not for ventricular FSI.
We propose one that adds blood flow to the monoventricular elastodynamics
benchmark of \citet{Arostica2025}: an idealised truncated-ellipsoid LV with an
analytical fibre field, a visco-active Holzapfel--Ogden myocardium and
pericardial Robin boundary conditions, whose cavity is filled with an
incompressible Newtonian fluid. Rather than a full cardiac cycle, two separate
problems are posed, without valves: passive filling through an inflow port,
and active contraction that ejects through an outflow port. Each is the FSI
counterpart of a solid-only case of \citet{Arostica2025}, for which nine
reference solutions exist, so the effect of the fluid can be isolated and
verified. Reference solutions are computed with the finite-volume toolbox
solids4foam. Every numerical setting is reported, and systematic studies of
mesh, time step, solid stabilisation, fluid schemes, interface conditions,
coupling acceleration and coupling tolerance establish both convergence (with
grid-convergence indices) and robustness.
\RES{key numbers: effect of the fluid on the AR25 displacements, converged
volumes, GCI, recommended settings.}
Geometry, meshes, case files and reference data are released openly, and a
minimum reporting checklist for ventricular FSI is proposed.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item Adds blood flow to the Ar\'ostica et al.\ (2025) cardiac elastodynamics benchmark.
\item Separate passive-filling and active-contraction FSI problems, no valves needed.
\item Each FSI problem has a solid-only counterpart with nine reference codes.
\item Every solver, stabilisation and coupling setting reported and varied.
\item Open meshes, case files, reference data and a reporting checklist.
\end{highlights}
