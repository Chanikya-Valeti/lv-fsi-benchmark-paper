\section{Reference numerical method}\label{sec:methods}

Reference solutions are computed with solids4foam \citep{Cardiff2018}
\TODO{version / commit} on OpenFOAM \TODO{v2312/v2412}. Every setting below is
also given in the released case files.

\subsection{Solid solver}
Cell-centred finite-volume, total-Lagrangian, total-displacement formulation
(\texttt{nonLinearGeometryTotalLagrangianTotalDisplacement}); law
\texttt{viscoActiveHolzapfelOgden}; Newton via PETSc SNES with a matrix-free
Jacobian and backtracking line search. Incompressibility: mixed
displacement--pressure formulation (\texttt{solvePressure true}) with
momentum stabilisation \texttt{diffStencilLaplacian} (scale factor 100) and
Rhie--Chow pressure stabilisation. \TODO{justify stabilisation factor;
sensitivity in \cref{sec:ver:schemes}.} Time integration: second-order
backward differentiation (BDF2) for $\ddot\uu$ and $\dot\uu$.

\subsection{Fluid solver}
PIMPLE pressure--velocity algorithm on a moving mesh with
\texttt{ddtCorr}; mesh motion by \TODO{Laplacian/velocity motion solver and
diffusivity}. Schemes in \cref{tab:schemes}.
