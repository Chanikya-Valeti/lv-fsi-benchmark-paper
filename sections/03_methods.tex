\subsection{Solid solver}\label{sec:methods:solid}
All values in this subsection are those of the pinned solid case
(\texttt{benchmark1} at commit \texttt{882cf07}), which is the inherited
baseline; the FSI runs may change the time step on the evidence of
\cref{sec:ver:time}.

\paragraph{Formulation} Cell-centred finite volume, total Lagrangian,
total-displacement formulation
(\texttt{nonLinearGeometryTotalLagrangianTotalDisplacement}), material law
\texttt{viscoActiveHolzapfelOgden}. Incompressibility: mixed
displacement--pressure formulation (\texttt{solvePressure true}), in which
the volumetric term $U'(J)$ of \cref{eq:Psi} is replaced by a cell-centred
pressure unknown $p_{\mathrm s}$ that satisfies the same penalty relation.
\CODECHECK{State the sign convention of $p_{\mathrm s}$ relative to
$U'(J)=\tfrac{\kappa}{2}(J-J^{-1})$, how it enters the stress, and the
discrete pressure equation.}

\paragraph{Stabilisation} Momentum: \texttt{diffStencilLaplacian} with
\texttt{scaleFactor}~100, i.e.\ the difference between a compact- and a
wide-stencil Laplacian of $\uu$, scaled by a stiffness and by $c_{\uu}=100$.
Pressure: Rhie--Chow with $c_p=1$ and $c_p^{\mathrm J}=1$ in the Jacobian.
The stiffness that scales the momentum stabilisation is the law's scalar
tangent, $\mu_{\mathrm{tan}} = a+(a_f+a_s+a_{fs})/6 = \SI{3587.2}{Pa}$ for the
baseline parameters, which excludes $\eta/\dt$ and is a small-strain
estimate rather than a tangent of the anisotropic energy.
\CODECHECK{Write the discrete stabilisation operators explicitly, with
units; state whether each factor enters the residual equations or only the
Jacobian/preconditioner; $c_p^{\mathrm J}$ in particular may affect solver
convergence only.}

\paragraph{Non-linear and linear solvers} PETSc SNES Newton with
backtracking line search (\texttt{newtonls}, \texttt{bt}), matrix-free
Jacobian (\texttt{snes\_mf}, \texttt{snes\_mf\_operator}),
\texttt{snes\_rtol} $10^{-6}$, \texttt{snes\_atol} $10^{-10}$; linear solver
LGMRES (restart 200, \texttt{ksp\_rtol} $10^{-4}$, at most 300 iterations)
with a Schur-complement field split (lower factorisation, \texttt{a11}
Schur preconditioner; block size 4, displacement fields 0--2, pressure
field 3), each block preconditioned once with HYPRE BoomerAMG (strong
threshold 0.6, one sweep up and down, one aggressive-coarsening level,
HMIS coarsening, \texttt{ext+i} interpolation, $P_{\max}=1$, truncation
0.3, at most 25 levels). The dictionary sets
\texttt{snes\_max\_linear\_solve\_fail} to $10^5$, a permissive policy on
failed linear solves; no SNES maximum iteration count is set.
\CODECHECK{Report the resolved SNES iteration limit and the actual
converged reasons of the production runs; set the limit explicitly in the
released case. The displacement predictor is commented out in the
dictionary; confirm its runtime behaviour.} The mixed formulation runs in
serial at the pinned commit; the displacement formulation runs in
parallel.

\paragraph{Time integration} \texttt{backward} (BDF2) for both $\dot\uu$
and $\ddot\uu$ (\texttt{ddtSchemes}, \texttt{d2dt2Schemes}). The viscous
stress uses a first-order difference of the Green--Lagrange strain over
the time step, $\dot\EE\approx[\EE^{n}-\EE^{n-1}]/\dt$ with
$\EE=\tfrac12(\FF^{\mathsf T}\FF-\II)$, so the temporal accuracy of the
solid is not formally second order; the observed order is measured, not
assumed (\cref{sec:ver:time}). \CODECHECK{Start-up of BDF2 at the first
step and the handling of old-time fields during FSI sub-iterations.}

\paragraph{Baseline time step and output} $\dt=\SI{1}{ms}$ on $[0,1]$\,s
(the inherited solid case); fields written every \SI{10}{ms} in ASCII with
8 significant digits; point displacements by \texttt{solidPointDisplacement}
at $\vect p_0$, $\vect p_1$ every step.

\subsection{Fluid--solid coupling}\label{sec:methods:coupling}
\paragraph{Algorithm} Partitioned and strongly coupled, Dirichlet--Neumann:
the fluid receives the interface velocity and the solid receives the fluid
traction. One time step proceeds as in \cref{alg:coupling}.
\CODECHECK{Verify every line of \cref{alg:coupling} against the pinned
\texttt{fluidSolidInterface} implementation, in particular the predictor,
where the mesh velocity is evaluated, when old-time fields are advanced,
and whether the residual is formed from the unrelaxed fixed-point update.}

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{Coupling sequence for one time step (to be verified against the
implementation).}
\label{alg:coupling}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lX}
\toprule
1 & Predict the interface displacement $\tilde\uu^{0}_{\Gfsi}$ from previous
    time steps (\TODO{order}). \\
2 & For $k=0,1,\dots$ until converged or $k=N_{\mathrm{FSI}}$: \\
2a & Move the fluid mesh to $\tilde\uu^{k}_{\Gfsi}$; compute $\ww$ and swept-volume fluxes. \\
2b & Solve the fluid problem with $\vv=\ww$ on $\Gfsi$ (velocity from the
    time scheme applied to $\tilde\uu^{k}_{\Gfsi}$). \\
2c & Evaluate $\sig_{\mathrm f}\nn_{\mathrm f}$ on the fluid side; map to the
    solid interface faces (\TODO{mapping; current or reference areas}). \\
2d & Solve the solid problem with this traction; obtain $\uu^{k+1}_{\Gfsi}$. \\
2e & Residual $r^{k} = \uu^{k+1}_{\Gfsi}-\tilde\uu^{k}_{\Gfsi}$ (unrelaxed fixed-point residual). \\
2f & Convergence test (\cref{eq:residual}); if not converged, accelerate:
    $\tilde\uu^{k+1}_{\Gfsi} = \tilde\uu^{k}_{\Gfsi}+\omega_k r^{k}$ (fixed or
    Aitken $\omega_k$) or the IQN-ILS update. \\
3 & Accept the step and advance old-time fields; or, on non-convergence,
    apply the failure policy (\cref{sec:methods:failure}). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\paragraph{Interface pressure condition} The pressure boundary condition
on the fluid side of $\Gfsi$ is \texttt{elasticWallPressure} with
\texttt{hsModel secant} \citep{Tukovic2018}, a Robin-type condition that
accounts for the local interface compliance to counter the added-mass
effect; blood and myocardium have similar densities
($\rho_{\mathrm f}/\rho_{\mathrm s}=1.05$), although geometry, constraints
and the structural response also govern the strength of the effect
\citep{Causin2005}. \CODECHECK{Write the implemented Robin equation and its
coefficients, and the secant model of the interface compliance, from the
pinned source.}

\paragraph{Convergence criterion} The tutorial sets
\texttt{outerCorrTolerance} $5\times10^{-5}$ and \texttt{nOuterCorr} 30. The
entries \texttt{robinPressureReference} ($0.1$, in the fluid pressure
units) and \texttt{robinFluxReferenceVelocity} (\SI{0.01}{m/s}) are lower
bounds on the scales used to normalise the pressure-change and
interface-flux residuals; they are stopping-criterion parameters, not Robin
impedance coefficients, and moving them changes the admissible absolute
residual rather than the physics. The displacement residual of the
implementation is \CODECHECK{exact norm, support (points or faces), area
weighting, reference scale and floor}; the form
\begin{equation}
\|r^{k}\|_{\mathrm{rel}} =
\frac{\|r^{k}\|_{2}}{\max\bigl(\max_{\Gfsi}\|\uu^{k+1}_{\Gfsi}\|,\;u_{\mathrm{floor}}\bigr)}
\le \epsilon_{\mathrm{FSI}}
\label{eq:residual}
\end{equation}
is a placeholder: an unweighted 2-norm scales with the number of interface
nodes and its denominator vanishes at rest, so the manuscript will report
the implemented definition and, if a mesh-independent diagnostic is added,
label it as a new diagnostic.

\paragraph{Acceleration} The tutorial uses \texttt{fixedRelaxation} with
$\omega=1$, i.e.\ no relaxation. The baseline for the reference runs will be
chosen from the pilot runs among fixed relaxation, Aitken dynamic
relaxation \citep{Kuttler2008} and IQN-ILS \citep{Degroote2009}
(\cref{sec:ver:robust}). \CODECHECK{Confirm that each method is available
and compatible with \texttt{elasticWallPressure} at the pinned version.}

\paragraph{Failure policy}\label{sec:methods:failure}
A time step that reaches $N_{\mathrm{FSI}}$ iterations without meeting
\cref{eq:residual} has not converged. \AUTHOR{Policy: terminate the run,
or halve $\dt$ and repeat the step (with a minimum $\dt$). A run that
reaches the end time with unconverged steps or failed inner solves is not a
successful reference run; the success criterion of
\cref{sec:ver:robust} requires completion \emph{and} convergence of every
step \emph{and} the conservation and mesh-quality checks.}
