\subsection{Limitations}
Idealised geometry without papillary muscles or trabeculae; no valve
leaflets; prescribed (not physiologically regulated) inflow/outflow;
Newtonian, laminar blood; prescribed rather than electrophysiology-driven
activation; the AR25 ventricle is large ($V_0=\SI{168}{mL}$ unloaded), more
representative of a dilated than a healthy LV. These are deliberate
simplifications that keep the benchmark unambiguous; each can be relaxed in
later steps.
