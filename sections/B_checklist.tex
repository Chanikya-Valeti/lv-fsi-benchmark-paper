\section{Minimum reporting checklist for ventricular FSI}\label{app:checklist}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Proposed minimum reporting checklist.}
\label{tab:checklist}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lX}
\toprule
Category & Items \\
\midrule
Geometry & Cavity and wall dimensions or the surface file; reference
  (unloaded vs.\ image) configuration; reference cavity volume \\
Fibres & Rule/algorithm, helix/sheet angles, data file \\
Solid model & Constitutive law and all parameters; incompressibility treatment;
  active model and activation timing; solid boundary conditions \\
Fluid model & Density, viscosity (rheology), turbulence assumption;
  inflow/outflow conditions and waveforms; valve treatment \\
Discretisation & Method (FE/FV/IB/\dots), element/cell type and order,
  cell counts and size, spatial schemes \\
Stabilisation & Type and factors for solid (momentum, pressure) and fluid
  (convection, pressure--velocity), and evidence that results are insensitive
  to them \\
Mesh motion & Motion solver, diffusivity, minimum cell quality reached \\
Time integration & Schemes for solid and fluid, $\dt$, Courant number \\
Coupling & Monolithic/partitioned; sub-iteration sequence and predictor;
  interface conditions and transfer; relaxation/acceleration; exact residual
  definition, normalisation and tolerance; iterations per step; failed-step
  policy \\
Forcing & Activation and pressure waveforms as equations and as the released
  tables actually used, with initial values \\
Verification & Mesh and time-step sensitivity per QoI with convergence classification and error estimates; conservation diagnostics; iterative-error check \\
Availability & Code commit, dependencies and patches, input files, meshes, settings manifest, output data with units and conventions, archival DOI \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
