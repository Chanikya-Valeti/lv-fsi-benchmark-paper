\section{Minimum reporting checklist for ventricular FSI}\label{app:checklist}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Proposed minimum reporting checklist.}
\label{tab:checklist}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lX}
\toprule
Category & Items \\
\midrule
Geometry & Cavity and wall dimensions or the surface file; reference
  (unloaded vs.\ image) configuration; reference cavity volume \\
Fibres & Rule/algorithm, helix/sheet angles, data file \\
Solid model & Constitutive law and all parameters; incompressibility treatment;
  active model and activation timing; solid boundary conditions \\
Fluid model & Density, viscosity (rheology), turbulence assumption;
  inflow/outflow conditions and waveforms; valve treatment \\
Discretisation & Method (FE/FV/IB/\dots), element/cell type and order,
  cell counts and size, spatial schemes, stabilisation \\
Time integration & Schemes for solid and fluid, $\dt$, Courant number \\
Coupling & Monolithic/partitioned, interface conditions, relaxation/acceleration,
  tolerance, iterations per step \\
Verification & Mesh and time-step sensitivity with QoIs and error estimates \\
Availability & Code version, input files, meshes, output data \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
