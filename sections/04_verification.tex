\section{Verification and robustness methodology}\label{sec:verification}

\subsection{Error estimation}
For a scalar quantity $\phi$ computed on three systematically refined meshes
(or time steps) with ratio $r$, the observed order, extrapolated value and
fine-grid convergence index are \citep{Roache1998,Celik2008}
\begin{equation}
p_{\mathrm{obs}} = \frac{\ln\bigl|(\phi_3-\phi_2)/(\phi_2-\phi_1)\bigr|}{\ln r},\qquad
\phi_{\mathrm{ext}} = \phi_1 + \frac{\phi_1-\phi_2}{r^{p_{\mathrm{obs}}}-1},\qquad
\mathrm{GCI}_{21} = \frac{1.25\,|(\phi_1-\phi_2)/\phi_1|}{r^{p_{\mathrm{obs}}}-1}.
\end{equation}
For time series we use the $\RED$ of AR25 against the finest solution and the
relative $L^2$-in-time norm
$e_{L^2} = \|\phi_h-\phi_{\mathrm{ref}}\|_{L^2(0,T)}/\|\phi_{\mathrm{ref}}\|_{L^2(0,T)}$.
Scalar QoIs for the GCI:
\begin{itemize}
\item Problem F: peak and final cavity volume, peak $\bar p$ (F-Q),
  peak $\Delta p_{\mathrm{IVPD}}$, peak $E_k$, $\uu(\vect p_0)$ and
  $\uu(\vect p_1)$ at the time of peak loading;
\item Problem C: ejected volume, peak outflow rate, peak $\Delta p_{\mathrm{IVPD}}$,
  peak $E_k$, $\uu(\vect p_0)$ and $\uu(\vect p_1)$ at peak contraction.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Sensitivity to numerical settings and robustness}\label{sec:ver:robust}
Starting from the baseline (\cref{tab:settings}), one setting is varied at a
time on mesh M2 (and M3 for the most influential). For each run we record
(i) whether it completes, (ii) the deviation of the QoIs from the baseline
($\RED$), and (iii) the cost: FSI iterations per step and wall-clock time.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Solid incompressibility}: mixed $\uu$--$p$ vs.\ pure
  displacement with penalty $\kappa$.
\item \textbf{Solid momentum stabilisation}: type (\texttt{RhieChow},
  \texttt{diffStencilLaplacian}) and factor
  $c_{\uu}\in\{0.1, 1, 10, 100\}$ \TODO{range}; show the trade-off between
  checkerboard suppression and artificial stiffening, and its dependence on
  mesh size (the stabilisation should vanish with $h\to0$).
\item \textbf{Solid pressure stabilisation}: $c_p \in \{0.1, 1, 10\}$.
\item \textbf{Fluid time scheme}: Euler vs.\ backward.
\item \textbf{Fluid convection scheme}: upwind, linearUpwind, linear.
\item \textbf{Mesh motion}: diffusivity choice; minimum cell quality reached
  at peak deformation.
\item \textbf{Interface condition}: Dirichlet--Neumann vs.\ Robin;
  Robin parameters $p_{\mathrm{ref}}$, $U_{\mathrm{ref}}$ over two orders of
  magnitude.
\item \textbf{Acceleration}: fixed relaxation ($\omega\in\{0.1,0.5,1\}$),
  Aitken, IQN-ILS (with reuse of past time steps).
\item \textbf{Coupling tolerance}:
  $\epsilon_{\mathrm{FSI}}\in\{10^{-3},10^{-4},10^{-5},10^{-6}\}$; the
  loosest tolerance whose QoIs agree with the tightest within the
  discretisation error is the recommended value.
\end{enumerate}
The outcome is a robustness map (\cref{tab:robust}) and a recommended
``benchmark-grade'' configuration.

\subsection{Conservation and consistency checks}
\begin{itemize}
\item cavity volume from the fluid mesh vs.\ from $\Gendo$, and (F-Q)
  vs.\ $V_0+\int_0^t Q_{\mathrm{in}}\,\mathrm ds$;
\item discrete geometric conservation law of the ALE scheme (a uniform
  flow is preserved on the moving mesh);
\item energy budget: power of the interface tractions vs.\
  $\dot E_k + \Phi$ plus port fluxes;
\item interface consistency: velocity and traction jumps across $\Gfsi$ at
  convergence.
\end{itemize}
